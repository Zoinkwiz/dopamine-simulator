# Dopamine Simulator

A RuneLite plugin that adds more progression systems on top of Old School RuneScape.

Everything you do earns Points. Points buy upgrades and card packs. Packs contain cards, and the
cards you collect get you more points.

<img width="1687" height="1039" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8d36c7bf-5d23-42fc-9e52-67be4dfc0995" />

## Getting Points

Start off clicking the gold button to get points. As you get points, you'll unlock new ways to get points, and ways to enhance the amount of points point-sources give.

<img width="351" height="822" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ea1cb8c6-22d1-4de7-998d-656e061c5bf3" />


## Card Packs

Use your points to also buy card packs. Different rarities, shinies, gilded, and also a star system so you have many progression systems in one bundle!

<img width="910" height="796" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/acd714a8-9d39-4164-9f3f-2cc03a6b3e72" />

## Limited Cards

Not every card is in the packs. The raid megarares only ever come off a banner, the region cards
only from finishing a season pass, and the skill mastery cards only from prestiging.

## Battle Pass

Do you crave time-limited reward systems that incentivise you to cram in lots of gaming? Lucky you, here's a battle pass system! Gain exp, unlock packs and rewards. You can spend your currency to unlock the premium track too.
